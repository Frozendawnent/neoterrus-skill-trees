How the trees work

Base tree
Always on. Abilities have 3 ranks and passives have 3 or 5. Each rank costs 1 point.
Tiers
Tiers open as you spend points in the base tree: 5, 10, 15, 20 and 25.
Augments and capstones
Augments (the diamonds) change one ability. You equip one augment per ability and one capstone.
Specializations
At 15 base points the gate opens both specialization trees. Unlock a tree’s Protocol to start spending there. Points can go into either one.
Active tree and spillover
One specialization is active for the playthrough, and its abilities can swap into your slots. Points in the other tree give a small spillover bonus: +1% per 2 points, up to 8%.
DLC
Each DLC planet’s power source adds a new tree in the locked slot.

All point costs, tier thresholds and spillover numbers are placeholders.
